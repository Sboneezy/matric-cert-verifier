# Vision

## Why this project exists

Verification agencies spend a significant amount of time performing repetitive administrative work.

A single verification request may require an operator to:

- Open each certificate
- Read candidate details manually
- Capture information into provider-specific templates
- Split large requests into provider-defined batch sizes
- Prepare emails
- Track responses
- Maintain audit records

These activities are repetitive, time-consuming and prone to human error.

---

## Our Mission

Build a platform that automates the administrative process of certificate verification while ensuring that humans remain in control of every submission.

Artificial Intelligence should assist operators—not replace them.

---

## Design Principles

- AI extracts information.
- Humans approve extracted data.
- Provider rules are configurable.
- Every action is auditable.
- Manual work should only exist where human judgement is required.

---

## Long-term Vision

Support multiple verification providers including:

- Umalusi
- SAQA
- Department of Education

Provide:

- AI-assisted extraction
- Dynamic template generation
- Batch processing
- Email automation
- Status tracking
- Audit history
- Reporting
- Multi-agency support
